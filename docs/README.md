Brief summary of OctoAcme project management processes:

OctoAcme follows an iterative, customer-first approach with clear ownership and lightweight artifacts. Projects start with a one-pager to confirm business need and success metrics, proceed to planning where work is broken into shippable increments with acceptance criteria and estimates, and move into execution with regular standups, a project board workflow, and CI-driven PR policies. Releases follow a checklist-driven deployment process with pre-release checks and a rollback plan. Risks and dependencies are tracked in a risk register and escalated via defined paths. After milestones or incidents, retrospectives capture action items and feed continuous improvements back into the backlog.
